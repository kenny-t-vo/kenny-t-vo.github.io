how this site is set.

This site has no build server or framework, and nothing running on the page except the spread viewers.

the faces.

Both faces are my own, made in October 2026 and served from this domain under the Open Font License.

Titles are set in Hiep Hoa 35. Hiep Hoa is Redaction, drawn by Forest Young and Jeremy Mickel for an exhibition at MoMA PS1, with the cuts in its serifs and terminals closed, its corners softened and the Vietnamese alphabet added. The 35 is a pixel grade, traced from those outlines on a 35-unit grid as Redaction’s own grades are. Spacing and kerning are Redaction’s.

Body text is Long An Light. It starts from STIX Two Text and is reproportioned by script toward Plantin MT Pro Light as I set it, at -10: the same x-height and colour, a little more refined and less airy, not as dense or sharp as Times.1 The letterforms are still STIX’s. Until the files load, Times New Roman stands in, sized to the same width and line height so nothing moves when they arrive.

Both names are places: Hiệp Hòa, a commune that kept its name when it absorbed its neighbours in 2025, and Long An, the province it belonged to, merged into Tây Ninh the same year.

Sizes come from six steps and nothing sits between them: 22, 16, 14, 12.5, 11 and 10.5 pixels. Spacing runs on a single rhythm. Both are variables, so a change is one edit.

the pages.

The two hyperlink colours are the browser defaults, #0000ee and #551a8b: blue is something you can open, purple something you have. Interface chrome is exempt, because a back link points at a page you have by definition already seen.

Hairlines separate blocks when necessary.

The portfolios are PDFs rendered to WebP at three resolutions, one to read, one to zoom into, one for the index, and their hyperlinks are lifted out of the PDF and laid back over the page, so a reference printed in blue is still a reference you can follow.

  • footnotes, which collect under a rule at the end
  • block quotes, lists, and tables
  • images with captions
